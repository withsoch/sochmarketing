## What does an optimised Google Business Profile actually do for a restaurant?

An optimised Google Business Profile improves your ranking on three factors Google uses directly: relevance (does your profile match what someone searched for), distance (how close you are to the searcher), and prominence (how complete, active and well-reviewed your profile is). Improve all three and you appear higher in Google Maps results, get more calls, more direction requests, and more clicks on your Wolt and Bolt Food order links, before a competitor even enters the picture.

93% of diners check Google before choosing a restaurant, and your Business Profile is the first thing they see, not your website. Google's own data shows a profile receives on average seven times more views than the restaurant's website. If your profile is incomplete, that traffic goes nowhere useful.

## Step 1: Claim, verify, and lock down your profile basics

Before anything else, confirm you own and have verified the profile. Unverified profiles can be edited by anyone, including Google's automated systems pulling wrong information from the web.

![Restaurant owner checking business details on a laptop at the counter](/blog/google-business-profile-restaurant-missing-fields-inline-1.jpg)

*Ten minutes checking your name, address and category can decide who Google shows first.*

Once you have access, check that your name, address and phone number match exactly across your Google profile, your own website, your Wolt listing and your Bolt Food listing. Exactly means the same spelling, the same abbreviations or lack of them, the same phone format. A mismatch signals inconsistency to Google and weakens your relevance score.

**The category mistake most Tallinn venues make is choosing "Restaurant" and leaving it there.** Your primary category should be as specific as possible: "Cafe", "Shisha Lounge", "Cocktail Bar", "Lunch Restaurant". A cafe near the office blocks in Ülemiste that picks "Restaurant" will lose ground to a competitor who picked "Cafe" when someone searches "cafe near me" at 9am. Add secondary categories too. You can add more than one, and they each expand the searches you can appear for.

Google confirms directly that [businesses with complete and accurate information are more likely to appear in local results](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en). This step costs nothing and takes under ten minutes.

## Step 2: Fill every field, the ones most owners skip

The business description, attributes and menu fields are where most profiles go half-finished. Each one changes which searches you appear for.

### Writing a business description that works in Estonian and English

Google gives you 750 characters for the description. Use the first 250 to say clearly what you are and what you offer, because that is all most people see before the "more" button. Write it so it reads naturally, not like a keyword list. For a Tallinn venue, write the description in English. Your customers searching in English, tourists, expats, and the growing number of locals who search in English, will read it. Separately, your posts and updates can go in Estonian first, then English, which covers both audiences without splitting the profile.

### Menus and attributes: the fields that change what searches you appear for

Do not upload a PDF menu. Google cannot read a PDF in any meaningful way for ranking purposes. Use the built-in menu editor and add individual dishes with names and short descriptions. A shisha lounge that lists flavours, a bar that names its cocktails, a lunch spot that adds its daily specials as menu items: all of these tell Google what the venue actually offers, which is how you start appearing for searches like "halal lunch Tallinn" or "hookah lounge Tallinn".

Attributes matter too. Outdoor seating, Wi-Fi, halal options, reservations accepted, good for groups. Each ticked attribute is a filter someone might use on Google Maps. If yours is blank and a competitor's is full, they get the click.

## Step 3: Photos and posts, how often and what to shoot

40% of diners visit a venue because of food photography they saw online. Photos uploaded by the owner give you control over the first impression. User photos appear too, but they are often badly lit, taken from the wrong angle, or showing a dish you no longer serve. Add your own and they sit at the front.

![Person photographing a plated dish on a restaurant table with a phone](/blog/google-business-profile-restaurant-missing-fields-inline-2.jpg)

*Photos you upload yourself decide the first impression, before a single guest walks in.*

At a minimum, upload these four types:

- **Exterior, daytime.** Helps people recognise the entrance when they are walking there.
- **Interior.** One wide shot that shows the atmosphere, whether that is a quiet Old Town cafe or a dimly lit shisha lounge.
- **Dish close-ups.** Your three or four best sellers, shot in natural light if possible.
- **Team.** One photo with staff makes the place feel real and approachable.

For posts, weekly is the practical minimum. A Tuesday special, a Friday event, a new menu item. Keep posts short and write them in Estonian first, then add a line in English. Most of your local regulars read Estonian; most tourists read English. One post covers both if you structure it that way.

## Step 4: Reviews, how to get them and how to reply in two languages

The best moment to ask for a review is right after the experience, when the customer is still at the table or just settling the bill. A short, genuine ask from a staff member converts far better than a bulk email sent three days later. Keep a QR code on the table that links directly to your Google review page.

Replying to every review is a Google-confirmed ranking signal, and it is also the part most Tallinn venues handle badly. A one-line copy-pasted reply does more harm than good. For a positive review left in Estonian, reply in Estonian. For one left in English, reply in English. For negative reviews, acknowledge the specific complaint, avoid being defensive, and close with an invitation to return. One honest reply to a bad review does more for your reputation than ten replies to good ones.

## Step 5: Read your GBP Insights and know what the numbers mean

Google Business Profile Insights shows you four numbers worth watching regularly: calls, direction requests, website clicks, and photo views. Together they tell you whether your profile is doing its job.

Direction requests are the most useful signal for a venue. A drop in direction requests usually means one of three things: your hours are wrong and people are finding you closed, a competitor has overtaken you in ranking, or your photos and description have stopped drawing people in. Check the other three metrics alongside it and the cause usually becomes obvious.

Photo views tell you which images are being seen most. If the top photo is a user-uploaded shot from two years ago, upload better ones and watch the number shift over the following weeks. Calls dropping on a specific day of the week can mean your hours for that day are listed wrong, which links back to Step 1.

## FAQ

### How do I get my restaurant to show up on Google Maps?

Claim and verify your Google Business Profile, choose a specific primary category rather than just "Restaurant", and fill in every available field including address, phone, hours, menu and attributes. Complete profiles rank higher because Google has more information to match against a searcher's query. Consistency between your Google listing and your Wolt and Bolt Food listings also helps.

### How often should a restaurant post on Google Business Profile?

Once a week is a realistic and effective minimum. Posts expire after a period, so a profile with no recent posts looks inactive to both Google and to anyone browsing it. Use posts for weekly specials, events or new dishes. For Tallinn venues, writing each post in Estonian and then adding a line in English means one post reaches both audiences.

### Do Google reviews affect a restaurant's local ranking?

Yes. Review volume, overall rating and how recently reviews have come in all feed into Google's prominence score, one of the three local ranking factors. Replying to reviews, in both Estonian and English where your audience uses both, is also a signal Google reads. A profile with regular new reviews and active replies consistently outranks one that is dormant.

Täpselt tähendab sama kirjaviisi, samu lühendeid või nende puudumist, sama telefoni formaati. Lahknevus annab Google'ile märku ebajärjekindlusest ja nõrgendab su asjakohasuse skoori.

**Kõige levinum kategooriaviga, mida Tallinna söögikohtades tehakse, on valida „Restoran" ja jätta see sinnapaika.** Peakategooria peaks olema võimalikult täpne: „Kohvik", „Vesipiibubaar", „Kokteilibaar", „Lõunarestoran". ## 2. samm: täida kõik väljad, sealhulgas need, mida enamik omanikke vahele jätab

Ettevõtte kirjeldus, atribuudid ja menüüväljad on need kohad, kus enamik profiile pooleli jääb. Igaüks neist mõjutab, millistes otsingutes sa ilmud.

### Kirjelduse kirjutamine, mis toimib nii eesti kui ka inglise keeles

Google annab sulle kirjelduseks 750 tähemärki. Kasuta esimest 250 selleks, et selgelt öelda, kes sa oled ja mida pakud, sest see on kõik, mida enamik inimesi enne „rohkem" nuppu näeb. Kirjuta nii, et loeks loomulikult, mitte nagu märksõnade loend. Tallinna söögikoha puhul kirjuta kirjeldus inglise keeles. Seda loevad inglise keeles otsivad kliendid – turistid, eksperdid ja üha kasvav hulk kohalikke, kes otsivad inglise keeles. Postitused ja uuendused võid kirjutada eesti keeles esimesena, seejärel inglise keeles – nii jõuad mõlema sihtrühmani ilma profiili lõhkumata.

### Menüüd ja atribuudid: väljad, mis muudavad, millistes otsingutes sa ilmud

Ära laadi üles PDF-menüüd. Google ei suuda PDF-i järjestamise mõttes sisuliselt lugeda. Kasuta sisseehitatud menüüredaktorit ja lisa üksikud road koos nimede ja lühikirjeldustega. Vesipiibubaar, mis loetleb maitseaineid, baar, mis nimetab oma kokteilid, lõunasöögipunkt, mis lisab oma päevapraed menüükaupadena – kõik need ütlevad Google'ile, mida koht tegelikult pakub, ja just nii hakkad ilmuma otsingutes nagu „halal lõunasöök Tallinn" või „vesipiibubaar Tallinn".

Atribuudid on samuti olulised. Õueterrass, Wi-Fi, halal-valikud, reserveerimine võimalik, sobib suurematele seltskonnadele. Iga märgitud atribuut on filter, mida keegi Google Mapsis kasutada võib. Kui sinu oma on tühi ja konkurendil täis, saab tema klikki.

## 3. samm: fotod ja postitused – kui tihti ja mida pildistada

40% söögikoha külastajaid läheb kohta, kuna nägi seal toidufotosid internetis. Omaniku lisatud fotod annavad sulle kontrolli esmamulje üle. Kasutajate fotod ilmuvad samuti, kuid need on tihti kehva valgusega, valest nurgast tehtud või näitavad rooga, mida sa enam ei paku. Lisa omad ja need kuvatakse esikohal.

![Inimene pildistab restoranilaualt telefongiga serveeritud rooga](/blog/google-business-profile-restaurant-missing-fields-inline-2.jpg)

*Ise lisatud fotod määravad esmamulje – enne kui ükski külaline sisse astub.*

Laadi üles vähemalt need neli tüüpi:

- **Välisilme päevasel ajal.** Aitab inimestel sissepääsu ära tunda, kui nad jalsi kohale tulevad.
- **Sisevaade.** Üks laiema vaatega foto, mis näitab atmosfääri – olgu selleks rahulik Vanalinna kohvik või hämaras vesipiibulounge.
- **Roogade lähivõtted.** Su kolm-neli enimmüüdud rooga, võimalusel looduslikus valguses pildistatud.
- **Meeskond.** Üks foto koos töötajatega muudab koha elulisemaks ja kutsuvamaks.

Postituste osas on üks kord nädalas praktiline miinimum. Teisipäeva päevapakkumine, reede üritus, uus menüükaup. Hoia postitused lühikesed ja kirjuta need eesti keeles, lisades seejärel rea inglise keeles. Enamik kohalikke püsikliente loeb eesti keeles, enamik turiste inglise keeles. Üks postitus jõuab mõlemani, kui see nii üles ehitada.

## 4. samm: arvustused – kuidas neid saada ja kuidas vastata kahes keeles

Parim hetk arvustuse küsimiseks on kohe pärast kogemust, kui klient on veel lauas või arvet tasumas. Töötaja lühike ja siiras palve toimib palju paremini kui kolm päeva hiljem saadetud massikirje. Hoia laual QR-kood, mis viib otse su Google'i arvustuste lehele.

Igale arvustusele vastamine on Google'i kinnitatud järjestamissignaal ja just see on osa, millega enamik Tallinna söögikohtade omanikke halvasti hakkama saab. Üherealiselt kopeeritud vastus teeb rohkem kahju kui kasu. Eesti keeles jäetud positiivsele arvustusele vasta eesti keeles. Inglise keeles jäetule vasta inglise keeles. Negatiivsete arvustuste puhul tunnista konkreetset probleemi, ära ole kaitsev ja lõpeta kutsega uuesti tulla. Üks aus vastus halvale arvustusele teeb su mainega rohkem kui kümme vastust headele.
